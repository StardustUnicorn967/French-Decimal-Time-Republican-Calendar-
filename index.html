How the system works

Republican unitModern equivalent

1 month30 calendar days

Calendar calculation: This app uses a clearly defined modern continuation of the Republican calendar, with every new year beginning on 22 September and 5 or 6 complementary days according to the number of days between consecutive year boundaries. The original historical system used the autumn equinox at Paris, so historical dates and some future boundary dates can differ from this simplified continuation. The historical Year I epoch was 22 September 1792; the 1793 option is a user-defined alternative. Time follows the chosen device/Paris/UTC civil day, including daylight-saving adjustments when applicable.
